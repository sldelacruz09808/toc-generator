Choose your brief (PDF)

Body of the brief only. In Word: File > Save As > PDF. Page 1 of the PDF counts as page 1 unless you change the starting page.

Add an authority it missed

Type how it should read. Put a phrase from the brief in “find pages with” and it counts every page where that phrase appears.

What gets counted. Every page where a case, statute, rule or provision is cited, including footnotes, short forms (Kaleikini, 129 Hawaiʻi at 467) and Id. after a case. Id. after a statute is not counted; the statute is normally named on the same page. Names used without a citation are counted only when the brief defines them, like “Nelson I”. Open “Where found” on any row to check the exact spots.

Clickable page numbers (optional)

Do this last. Choose the Word file your PDF was saved from. The builder adds the table to a copy of it, and every page number becomes a link that jumps to that page in the PDF. Your original file is not changed.

Where should the table go? Type [[TOA]] on its own line in your Word file. If there is none, the table goes at the very top.
