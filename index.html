Dunn’s Table of Contents Generator

Runs in this browser tab. Your PDF is read on this computer and is never uploaded.

1. Choose your brief (PDF)

Write the brief in Word, save it as a PDF, then drop the PDF here.

2. Check the headings

Fix wording, level or page number. Unticked lines stay out of the table.

No.HeadingPage(s)Level

3. Your Table of Contents

Letter size, 1″ margins, Times New Roman 12 pt.

The Word file has real dot-leader tab stops, so you can still edit it. Opens in Word as a .docx.

4. Clickable page numbers (optional)

Do this last. Choose the Word file your PDF was saved from. The app adds the Table of Contents to a copy of it, and every page number becomes a Ctrl + click link to its heading. Your original file is not changed.

Where should the table go? Type [[TOC]] on its own line in your Word file. If there is none, the table goes at the very top, followed by a page break.
